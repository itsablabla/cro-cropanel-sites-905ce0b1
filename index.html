Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden or Surprise Fees
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects feel misled and distrustful when unexpected charges are revealed late in the purchasing process, making it difficult to assess the true cost upfront.
Unclear Refund and Cancellation Policies
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Ambiguous or poorly communicated refund and cancellation terms create uncertainty and perceived risk for prospects, potentially deterring them from completing a purchase.
Difficulty in Securing Service/Booking
Super smug and seem delighted to turn you away even when you try reserving weeks in advance. Even 2 weeks out, apparently, there is no room unless you want to eat lunch at 3 pm.
— embedsocial.com
Prospects experience frustration and perceive exclusivity when they encounter significant hurdles or limited availability while attempting to book or reserve a service.
Misleading Advertised Prices
Misleading advertised prices with high shipping costs
— deceptive.design
Prospects feel deceived when the initial price presented is not transparent and does not accurately reflect the final cost, eroding trust before they commit to a purchase.
Confusing Pricing Structures
Other ways some businesses tend to overcomplicate pricing include hidden fees, inconsistent pricing across channels, frequent price changes, convoluted bundling, and confusing loyalty programs.
— netsuite.com
Overly complex pricing, bundling, or loyalty programs make it difficult for prospects to understand the value and compare options, leading to hesitation and abandonment.
Misunderstanding Insurance Coverage
They messed my insurance copayment information; they charged me co-payment where my insurance cover full.
— embedsocial.com
Prospects expect their insurance to cover specific costs, and a lack of clarity or miscommunication before purchase leads to unexpected charges and dissatisfaction.
